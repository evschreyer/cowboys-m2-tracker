Method

How these numbers were made

  1. Data: every ACHA men's game since 2021-22 from ACHA's public stats feed (HockeyTech), including M1-vs-M2 games, plus box scores for shots, power plays and goalies.
  2. M1 rank at game time: ACHA's M1 computer ranking rebuilt from games before each date. Two ratings, goal differential capped at 7 and at 1, every OT/shootout game counted as a tie, averaged by rank. Checked against 13 published ACHA rankings: same top 10 every time, 90% of teams within one place.
  3. Pre-game odds: every ACHA men's team rated on one goal scale from games before the date, anchored to last season. Home ice +0.33 goals, game-to-game spread 3.0 goals, and M1 teams get +0.65 goals against M2 teams. That correction exists because M1 teams have beaten M2 teams more often than the shared scale predicts in each of the last five seasons, though the gap is shrinking.
  4. Checking the odds: tested on cross-division games using only information available before each game. When the model said 25%, teams won 20% of the time; at 45%, 51%; at 85%, 80%.
  5. Limits: shots are not expected goals (shot quality isn't in the data), samples against top-5 M1 teams are small, and early-season ratings rely heavily on last season.
