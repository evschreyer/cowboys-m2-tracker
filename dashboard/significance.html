Method and cautions

Read this before quoting a number

  1. Strength: every ACHA men's team rated in goals on one scale from all games (including cross-division), anchored to last season and calibrated on 825 cross-division games (log loss 0.534 vs 0.656 baseline). M1 teams get +0.65 against M2 teams, matching how M1 has outperformed the shared scale.
  2. Uncertainty: a team's rating has a standard deviation of 3.0 ÷ √(games + 2). With 15 games OSU's is about ±0.73 goals, so claims against teams within about a goal can't be settled yet.
  3. Multiple comparisons: history ranks compare OSU with 890 M2 team-seasons; a few would look exceptional by chance alone. Repeated patterns (two straight seasons at the top of "beating expectations") carry more weight than any single rank.
  4. Partial season: OSU's numbers are to date. At the same 15-game point, Lindenwood 2025-26 rated slightly higher than OSU does now.
  5. 2021-22 has no prior season to anchor ratings and is left out of cross-season comparisons.
