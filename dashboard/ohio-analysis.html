    Method

    Models and data

    1. Data: every ACHA men's game since 2021-22 (ACHA's HockeyTech feed), with box scores for every M1–M2 game, every OSU game and every 2026-27 game involving an M1 team.
    2. M1 rank at the time: our rebuild of ACHA's M1 computer ranking from games before each date (two ratings with goal differential capped at 7 and at 1, every OT/shootout game a tie, averaged by rank). Across 13 published ACHA rankings: same top 10 every time, 90% of teams within one place. Before Nov. 1, ACHA's official M1 ranking is a coaches' poll, so early-season ranks here are the rebuild.
    3. Shared-scale strength: every ACHA men's team rated in goals from all games before a date, anchored to last season (counted as 2 games, regressed 0.6 toward the division mean). Home ice is worth +0.33 goals, and M1 teams get +0.65 against M2 teams because M1 teams have beaten M2 teams more often than the shared scale implies in every season since 2022-23 (that edge is shrinking).
    4. Win probability: normal margin with a spread of 3.0 goals; tied margins split. Tested on 825 cross-division games: log loss 0.534 vs 0.656 for a constant-rate baseline, and well calibrated (predicted 25% → 20% actual, 45% → 51%, 85% → 80%).
    5. Weekend breakdown: "shot-based expectation" applies each team's season save percentage to the other team's shots. Shots are not shot quality, so goaltending and finishing credits are descriptive, not proof of skill or luck.
    6. Projections: win counts use the product of game-by-game probabilities (Poisson-binomial) with today's strengths; they don't update as games are played.
