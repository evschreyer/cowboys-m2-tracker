How ACHA ranks M2

Rebuilt from ACHA's own published ranking spreadsheets and raw-data sheets (2024-25 and 2025-26), then checked against all nine 2025-26 releases.

Game GD    = goals for − goals against, capped at ±7
GmPerf     = average Game GD (M2 vs M2 games only)
Sched      = average rating of opponents
Rating     = GmPerf + Sched   (recursive; league average fixed at 7.00)
OT adj     = 0.5 × OT losses ÷ games played
AWP        = 0.8 × W ÷ (W + L)
Final      = Rating + OT adj + AWP − penalty
Ranked by Final within each region

Rules from the 2026-27 ACHA Manual

  • Ranked by computer by region, using strength of schedule, win-loss percentage and goal differential (Part Five, Art. IV §1).
  • Only games against other M2 teams count. Games against M1 or M3 teams do not.
  • M2 vs M2 games that stay tied after 3-on-3 OT go to a shootout, so there are no ties. The shootout winner is credited one goal.
  • A league playoff game that goes to OT counts as a tie (§3C).
  • Forfeited games are left out of the math. The penalty is 0.25 per ineligible player per game, or per forfeit (§5).
  • Teams must be in good standing to be ranked. ACHA publishes the top 20 per region.
  • Postseason needs at least 16 M2 games and 18 ACHA games by the final ranking (§2A).
  • Top 2 per region go straight to Nationals. Seeds 3-16 play Regionals, and league auto-bids replace the lowest seeds.

What this can't see

  • Scores that teams have not entered yet. ACHA's own data has the same gap until the Monday reporting deadline.
  • Penalties for ineligible players and good-standing decisions, until ACHA announces them.
  • Ranking Committee decisions, which are final.

Backtest vs official 2025-26

Rating error = average gap between our rating and ACHA's. Most of it comes from scores that differ between the public feed and ACHA's file, not from the formula. "Top 2" counts how many of the eight automatic Nationals bids we placed correctly.
